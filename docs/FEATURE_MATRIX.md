## 2. Decision Summary: Reuse vs. Adapt vs. Rebuild

### Systems to Reuse Directly (Proven, Rock-Solid Foundation):
- **Keycaps 3D Geometry (`keycaps.glb` & `bongo-frames.glb`):** The procedural 3x3 box-sliced mesh is mathematically brilliant and handles arbitrary grid sizes.
- **Adaptive Graphics Frame Governor (`use-adaptive-graphics.ts`):** Crucial for mobile performance and low-end hardware safety.
- **Lenis Smooth Scroll + GSAP ScrollTrigger Integration:** Guarantees locked 60 FPS scroll interpolation.
- **Particle System (`Particles.tsx`):** Lightweight, elegant background ambiance.

### Systems to Adapt (Logic Kept, Tailored to Mayank):
- **3D Keyboard Config & Key Mapping:** Switch from Naresh's skillset to Mayank's verified AI/ML and modern engineering stack.
- **Motion Timelines (`motion.ts`):** Tune rotation quaternions, trigger thresholds, and responsive positioning.
- **Keycap Sound System (`use-keyboard-sounds.ts`):** Add an accessible mute toggle in the navigation header.
- **Experience Timeline:** Reframe as Education, Hackathon Achievements, and Technical Milestones.

### Systems to Rebuild (Custom Design & Verified Data):
- **Hero Section:** High-impact typography showcasing Mayank Dabas, his credentials at USAR GGSIPU, and verified projects.
- **Projects Section:** Custom cards with real, verified screenshots for FORENSIC-X, Suraksha Sense, Stivium IDE, REDZONE, and EventTwin.
- **Data Configuration Layer:** Clean, strictly typed data files (`config.ts`, `skills.ts`, `projects.ts`, `education.ts`) ensuring zero hardcoded text in components.
